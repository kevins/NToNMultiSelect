N:N Multi-Select names copy: steps for your tables

The names copy plug-in comes with its Associate and Disassociate steps only: the host columns follow links and unlinks made anywhere. To make them also follow deletes and renames of the linked records, this page creates the plug-in's Delete and Update steps for your linked tables only (the tables whose names are copied into a host column). The deletes and updates of every other table pay nothing.
